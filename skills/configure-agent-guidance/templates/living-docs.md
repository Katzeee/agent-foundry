## Living Docs

Keep every document **living**: both its content and its continued existence serve a purpose it has now. When the facts change, rewrite the claim in place rather than parking a newer claim beside the stale one.

Past material belongs where it still supports a present claim, or where preserving that record is itself the document's purpose. Revision narration that merely explains how the document reached its current form belongs to neither.

Documentation written to carry temporary work is **scaffolding**: keep it while it serves that purpose. Once the purpose ends, fold whatever still matters into the documents that own it, then remove the scaffolding. Before finishing, account for every temporary document this work touched: each either still serves an active purpose or is gone, with anything durable carried forward first.
